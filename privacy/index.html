Privacy policy

AI Face: Aging & Face Editor (com.krmndigital.aiface) · KRMN Digital · effective 10 November 2026

Summary: AI Face has no account system and no server. Every photo you edit, every face it finds and every result it creates is processed on your phone and stays there unless you share or save it yourself. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which processes some data as described below; AI Face Pro removes the ads.

Photos and faces

  • The photos you open, the edits you make (stored as a list of settings), the pictures the app computes from them (face landmarks, hair/skin/background masks, body pose) and your saved looks.
  • Face landmarks, masks and pose are computed by AI models that run on the device. They are used only to apply the edits you choose (for example retouch, makeup, age filter, background change) and are never sent anywhere, never used to identify anyone, never used for advertising and never shared with us or any third party.
  • These files live in the app's private storage, are excluded from Android backups and are deleted when you delete an edit in the app or uninstall it. Pictures you save go to your phone's gallery (Pictures/AI Face), where your own gallery and backup settings apply.
  • The app opens photos through Android's system photo picker (it only sees the photos you pick, it has no access to the rest of your gallery) or takes them with the in-app camera when you allow camera access.

Children

AI Face is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Delete an edit in "My edits", or all edits in Settings; uninstalling deletes everything.
  • Change your ad privacy choices in Settings (where required), reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, or buy Pro to remove ads.
