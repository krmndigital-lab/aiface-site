Frequently asked

  • Are my photos uploaded? No. All AI runs on your phone; the app works in airplane mode.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? In the app: Settings → Manage subscription, or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • I bought Pro on another phone. Sign in with the same Google account and tap Settings → Restore purchases.
  • A result looks wrong. Settings → Report a problem with a result. Lower the strength slider for a more natural look.
